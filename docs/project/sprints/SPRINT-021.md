# Sprint 021 — Integrated Engine V1 usefulness and operational acceptance

Status: **PLANNED**
Milestone: **Engine V1 — accepted scope before UX/acquisition work**
Dependency: [Sprints 015–020](SPRINT-020.md), each with separate source, quality
and admission receipts or explicit optional-method disposition.

## Outcome

Demonstrate a working end-to-end engine in the existing Kajo application, with
useful own-scale expected ratings, ongoing learning and honest limits. Publish
an auditable V1 capability receipt, then hand off to UX/acquisition, later UI and
release under ROADMAP. The 51-part vision is preserved beyond this bounded V1.

## Scope and deliverables

1. Assemble exact code/model/feature/policy/calibration/source/index versions and
   the compatible rollout/rollback bundle. Reconcile installed migrations and
   runtime settings; never replay completed resets or catalogue operations.
2. Exercise one real path: exposure → list save/rating/SKIP → committed evidence →
   state update → fresh recommendation → later experience/rating → frozen-error
   evaluation → bounded replay/challenger decision. Include BOOK and MOVIE,
   continuing Taste/Discovery and scoped Shared evidence.
3. Run a prospectively declared usefulness window using existing consented test
   access. Separate familiar-item Taste evaluation from outcomes of new actual
   recommendations. Keep label availability, experience completion, response
   selection and exposure limits explicit. Do not equate offline ranking error
   or shadow comparisons with causal policy uplift.
4. Compare against the locked useful baseline under the common metric protocol.
   For a randomized admitted policy test, record actual assignment/propensities,
   support and clustered analysis; otherwise restrict claims to observed predictive
   performance. Do not broaden public distribution to collect data prematurely.
5. Verify end-to-end p50/p95, state-update/freshness and resource budgets on the
   intended runtime/device, including long sessions, buffering, offline recovery,
   expiry, corrections and multi-Profile use. Separate measured server and phone
   components; simulated host timing is not a device result.
6. Exercise permission/retention/deletion, artifact withdrawal, retry safety,
   fallback, restore/rebuild, alerting and manual rollback. Verify personal and
   group boundaries across the complete loop.
7. Demonstrate that the admitted reusable computation can run through the Kajo
   adapter and existing non-media contract fixture without UI/auth/provider
   dependencies. A second real non-media product/quality study remains EB-12's
   later gate; the synthetic fixture proves interface portability only.
8. Produce section/EB coverage with IMPLEMENTED, ADMITTED, REJECTED METHOD,
   INSUFFICIENT or RESEARCH DEFERRED, exact evidence and next dependency for every
   item. Update relevant canonical owners; do not tick whole later generations.

## Required tests and acceptance

- All applicable code/native checks and exact-version compatibility gates pass;
  no unresolved evidence, cross-Profile or preservation defect remains.
- A declared model wins or meets its predeclared useful-baseline admission rule
  on untouched real outcomes with required precision, coverage and domain/style
  slices. Report prospective outcome mean and rating prediction separately;
  cross-user raw rating levels are never pooled as universal satisfaction.
- A predeclared noninferiority/guardrail check passes for covered subgroups,
  missingness, latency and lifecycle. Insufficient sample precision remains
  insufficient; adding observations follows the predeclared sequential protocol.
- Current-user updates and original forecast preservation are observed through
  the installed application, including retained buffered-card origins.
- Cold-start fallback remains usable; unsupported optional cohort/transfer/
  Working/dream channels stay off with explicit reasons and re-entry criteria.
- One operating evaluation cycle and a tested rollback are recorded. Monitoring
  distinguishes model drift from missing/late evidence and service failure.

## Stop conditions and handoff

Correctness and meaningful numerical-prediction quality are mandatory V1 gates.
Missing owner/device observations or insufficient real outcomes keep the exact
acceptance open; the next task is their bounded collection or a revised model,
not a fictional pass. Optional methods may be rejected without preventing a
useful simpler V1. No universal psychological certainty, long-term causal claim
or full five-generation completion is required.

After Engine V1 acceptance, ROADMAP schedules UX and acquisition (including web
Taste, anonymous identity/conversion, sharing and social flows), then visual UI
completion, then beta/store/release acceptance. Minimal test/measurement controls
inside engine work do not promote those larger product stages ahead of V1.
Public Share Link Gate and all release requirements remain separate.

## Evidence and completion rules

Apply the [Engine V1 quantitative protocol](../ENGINE_BUILDOUT.md#engine-v1-quantitative-protocol)
and keep separate design, source/CI, offline quality, hosted admission and observed
runtime statuses. Current actions are list saving (interest), numeric rating
(experienced Item) and contextual SKIP with unknown reason. SKIP supplies no
numeric rating or inferred dislike/recognition; preserve old stored semantics.
Do not add an En tunne control or require a separate consumed action.

Aggregate V1 acceptance is `MVP-ALG-012`. Each implementation issue names its
bounded deliverable under #302 and reuses
#336/#232 where applicable. One active packet is recorded in STATUS. Required
source checks, relevant native tests and changed-surface observations apply;
a passing fixture alone does not establish predictive usefulness. Correctness,
source withdrawal and authorization failures block admission. Rejected optional
methods retain a working baseline and a written decision; an unmet mandatory
quality gate stays open. A missing physical-device observation is recorded and
does not become an invented pass.
